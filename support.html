Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report an out-of-order bathroom?

Tap the bathroom marker on the map, then look for the "Report Incident" button. You can flag it as Out of Order or Private instantly.

Is my data anonymous?

Yes! We don't use real names. Your "Buddy" identity is linked to your secure login (Apple or Google), but other users only see your chosen avatar and nickname.

Why do I see an ad before directions?

Bathroom Buddies is free to use! Ads help us cover the costs of the map servers and data verification so we can keep the community running.
